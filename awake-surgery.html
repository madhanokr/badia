HomeAwake surgery

Stay awake. Go somewhere else.

For many hand and wrist operations you don’t need to be put to sleep. With WAVR, your hand is numbed, you put on a virtual reality headset, and you can talk to me while I work. Then you eat lunch.

WAVR: Wide Awake Virtual Reality
Your day

A surgery day that feels like a normal day.

WAVR combines WALANT, wide-awake local anesthesia with no tourniquet, a technique with more than twenty years of results in hand surgery worldwide, with immersive virtual reality made for the operating room.

Nogeneral anesthesia, breathing tube or grogginess
30–60%less costly, by the center’s own figures

  1. Before

    Eat lunch. Take your medicines.

    No fasting from midnight, and no stopping your usual medications unless Dr. Badia tells you to.

  2. Numbing

    Numb only the hand

    A local anesthetic is placed where the operation will be. No general anesthesia, no breathing tube, no grogginess afterward.

  3. Headset on

    Go somewhere else

    You explore a calm, realistic environment while the procedure happens. It’s most effective for the patients who are most anxious.

  4. During

    Talk to Dr. Badia

    You stay aware the whole time and can speak with him during the procedure.

  5. After

    Eat again, go home

    You can eat immediately afterward and recover fully alert.
