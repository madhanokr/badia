WAVR: Wide Awake Virtual Reality
Your day

A surgery day that feels like a normal day.

WAVR combines WALANT (wide-awake local anesthesia, no tourniquet), a technique with more than twenty years of results in hand surgery worldwide, with immersive virtual reality made for the operating room.

  1. Eat lunch. Take your medicines.

    No fasting from midnight, and no stopping your usual medications unless Dr. Badia tells you to.

  2. Numb only the hand

    A local anesthetic is placed where the operation will be. No general anesthesia, no breathing tube, no grogginess afterward.

  3. Put on the headset

    You explore a calm, realistic environment while the procedure happens. It’s most effective for the patients who are most anxious.

  4. Talk to Dr. Badia

    You stay aware the whole time and can speak with him during the procedure, and he can ask you to move a finger to check the repair.

  5. Eat again, go home

    You can eat immediately afterward and recover fully alert.

Awake with WAVR, or asleep?

Not every patient or procedure is a candidate. For those who are, here is how the day compares.

General anesthesiaWAVR, awake
Before surgeryFasting, and some medicines stoppedEat lunch and take your usual medicines
DuringUnconscious, with anesthesia risksAwake, calm, talking with your surgeon
AfterGrogginess, nausea for some, waiting to eatEat right away, fully alert
Infection riskStandardFewer infections reported
CostHigher30 to 60% less costly

Figures as published by Badia Hand to Shoulder Center. Studies cited by the center found carpal tunnel release in a hospital’s main operating room costs almost four times as much as in an ambulatory setting.
